Comment 1: In your tables you are showing not just an increase in lung resection numbers (281%) but also a 135% increase in the number of cases referred for surgical evaluation and almost 21% increase in the surgical resection rate.Such impressive numbers should be not just a consequence of team improvements but also the circuit of case referrals and decision-making was probably improved in any way.I believe your manuscript would be improved if you briefly describe the population you are covering, lung cancer incidence in your settings and were from you are receiving lung cancer patient for surgical therapy.If your process of change included any kind of agreements or standardisation with external or internal providers of patients, and medical staff in charge of postoperative care, ICU, etc.
