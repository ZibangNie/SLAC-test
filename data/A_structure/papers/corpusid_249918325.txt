The current most widely used way of image quality estimation relies heavily on the subjective assessment, while majority of past objective estimation methods are not satisfactory on accuracy. To solve them and realize unsupervised image quality estimation with high precision, this paper creates a linear way with "Proportional Partition" controlled by horizontal and vertical rates of extracted pixel to get best representations of the image with patching, balance the uneven distribution of image quality in each source image, and offer dynamic compatibility to images with high resolution. Besides, it estimates the image quality automatically with a model trained by current best artificial intelligence (AI) algorithm for target detection YOLOv4 with 1000 images random selected from ImageNet2013 database. The proposal also uses the spirit of joint indices from the current widely used method named Video Multimethod Assessment Fusion (VMAF). But we replace its Visual Information Fidelity (VIF) with Visual Saliency-induced Index (VSI) and add VSI to our target function because of VIF's dependence on subjective assessment, and also for VSI's better performance surpassing most recent IQA estimators as TOP3 best model in recent world. Besides, contrast masking is also included by objective function for the KL-divergence to simulate the human visual perception better. A creative "Batch Learning" way is found to address patches for less calculation and faster speed. All source images are pretreated with colour space transformation and normalization to improve descriptiveness of images and reduce the redundant points, and a threshold is devised to formulate suppression mechanisms. The proposed solution is tested to be a good image quality assessor in many aspects such as correctness, consistency, linearity, monotonicity and speed, and performs well on even HD images.

Introduction

Facing the exponentially growing flood of images and videos, how to make the solutions more automatic, precise, smarter and faster? This is a big challenge for computer visual researchers.

The first key challenge is to raise the accuracy of image quality assessment (IQA) to approach human visual system (HVS). It's found that undistorted reference image is easier to approximate the quality of an observed image by quantifying pixel-wise differences which lead the majority of IQA researches. However, in reality, images are hardly to be absolutely pure without noise. And different people may hold inverse views to the IQA of the same image; even the same person may give different scores when he is in different mode. Thus, the subjective perception became the focus of the field. But it is not reliable. So, machine learning approaches are introduced to the realm gradually.

Paper [1] proposes a solution which is composed of distortion types on picture attributes and degradation quantification with vector regression stage. Meanwhile, Paper [2] provides a black-box quality assessing way basing on neural network. Article [3] suggests a filter learning based assessment DMCIT 2022 Journal of Physics: Conference Series 2289 (2022) 012020 IOP Publishing doi: 10.1088/1742-6596/2289/1/012020 2 approach, in which a vector based regression solution is originated to find the best weights, and the filtered database is studied with a stochastic gradient descent (SGD) method through k-means clustering. A sparse coding method [4] introduces the abstract expression of images and quantifying distortion based on Spearman correlation. The sparse weight matrix is created by the independent component analysis (ICA), and thus, the ICA basic functions with k-fold validation are gained. The writer of paper [5] uses an unsupervised dictionary and a supervised learning method to regress. K-means clustering is used to learn the distorted images so as to produce a dictionary, and map features are scored with regression. In paper [6], the authors invents a way of unsupervised learning to gain quality-aware features out of distorted sources, and quality scores are calculated by regression. Besides these approaches basing on dictionaries, to obtain descriptive characteristics of images, the approaches research spatial and frequency information and supervised ways of regressing these features are innovated to assess image quality[7], [8], [9]. But all the algorithms above require the scores judged by human on the IQA, or even they don't need subjective help, they need the sources photos pretreated by special distortions. So, the UNIQUE [10] proposes an estimation perceiving quality through sparse representations learned by training with L-BFGS from generic image databases.

Deep learning became popular on IQA. It boosted the development of NR-IQA. Le Kang group [11] realized good NR-IQA effect with a five-layered CNN with 32×32 patches. Ke Gu team [12] transformed RGB to YIQ color space and extracted 3000 features from the new images for training the automatic sparse coder with L-BFGS. Their result testified DNN is a promising direction of IQA. Simone Bianco innovated DeepBIQ [13] model to train plentiful blocks of the source image but not the source itself with a fusion of different features and results on CNN, its LCC scores 0.91 which approaches the HVS far better than traditional methods. By modifying the backpropagation of Siamese network, XiaLei Liu raised RankIQA [14] to solve the insufficiency of IQA source data. Rank IQA obtained outstanding NR-IQA result better than the formers like NR-IQA and even the latest FR-IQA.

However, effect of training algorithm affects the final accuracy, compatibility and variance of the assessment results of estimators. The state of the art AI algorithm is YOLOv4 has accuracy 43.5% and 65FPS, which has the best AI ability, and it has the best stability. Thus, this paper proposes to use YOLOv4 to train the model together with the spirit of joint indices referred from VMAF. For the main part of estimation, we still succeed the UNIQUE after improving its shortages. So, we invent the equally portioned blocks and extract general feature of each block with according feature patch under the control of the dynamic rates of extracted pixels. This solution achieves the general balance of fusing various factors which should be taken into consideration, and thus produces best result.


Preprocessing

Each image should be carefully preprocessed to enhance the ability of transferring characteristics of the source image. The general steps of processing are indicated in Figure 1. Firstly, a color space selection is done and the selected image is subdivided into equally distributed blocks non-overlapping as Figure 2 according to the dynamically controlled horizontal and vertical rates of extracted pixels Rh and Rv respectively. Later, the Mp×Np squares located in the center of the blocks are chosen to be the patches sampled to represent the source image, in this paper we recommend Mp=Np=8. That is, we use 8×8 square as the patch in this paper though this is actually Then, each patch with three channels is simplified to be a 1-D vector formed by the 8×8×3=192 pixels, and the value of each pixel is added with the mean of the block channel by channel before the patch is transformed to be 1-D vector. All the normalized new 1-D patches will form the prepared matrix of sparse representation for next step.


Selecting the colour space

The visual perception of human being is most sensitive to the luminance indicated by Green color [15] [16]. However, normal color channels contain information on both intensity and chroma, and color channels are highly correlated with each other. So, RGB images are usually transformed into YCbCr format. Then, Y channel, which includes structural information [17], is used specially. Furthermore, according the research, Cr channel are also necessary in transferring lightness.


Partition controlled by Rates of Extracted Pixels

The qualities of different parts of the real images are usually unevenly distributed. Thus, an orderly arranged patching way can help to grasp and transfer the key features to the estimators.

Besides, the normal method of fixing the quantity of patches sampled for each image is not flexible and sufficient for images with high resolution. For example, the solution of UNIQUE [10] samples 100 patches randomly from each source image. 100 patches seem to be narrowly ok for images with low definition like 512×512 images, and its rate of extracted pixel would be (8×8×100)/(512×512)=2.44%. But for HD pictures whose resolution is normally 1280×720, FHD pictures which is 1920×1080, 4Kpictures which is 4096×2160, 5K pictures which is 5120×2880, or even 8K pictures 7680×4320, 100 8×8 patches can extract at most 0.695%, 0.308%, 0.072%, 0.043%, 0.019% pixels from the source image when the patches are all non-overlapping. Obviously, it's hardly enough to represent the source images with a rate of extracted pixel less than one 1%. More extracted pixels and higher rates of extraction for images, are necessary for better sparse representation. So, dynamic quantity of extracted pixels adjusted automatically is better.

This paper suggests a proportionally partition way which divides the source image into Mb×Nb non-overlapping blocks within which there is only one Mp×Np sampled patch in the center (Character b stands for block and character p stands for patch. Mb, Nb, Mp, Np are integers, and Mp≤Mb, Np≤Nb) as shown in Figure 2. When Mb==Mp and Nb==Np, the patch's region equals to the region of the block it is in, so the block and its patch completely coincide. Here we suggest to set Mp=Np=8.

We also use Rh and Rv to dynamically control the quantity of the blocks or patches respectively:
Qh = |M×Rh|/Mb, Qv = |N×Rv|/Nb(1)
Here |X| gives the closest whole positive integer less than X. Since there are probably pixels which are out of the left right Sample Rectangular whose sampled width and height are Qh×Mb and Qv×Nb respectively (The "Sample Rectangular" is shown in Figure 2 as the left right rectangular drawn with red lines), these left blocks whose length is shorter than regular standard Mb×Nb are indicated as "Irregular Blocks" or "Useless Blocks" because they'll not be sampled. By contrast, the blocks in red within the sample rectangular are "Regular Blocks" who are useful for sampling.

Then, the left pixels of the useless blocks in horizontal and vertical blocks are as following:
Lh = M -Qh×Mb, Lv = N -Qv×Nb(2)
Obviously, Lh and Lv are integers, and Lh≤Mb, Lv≤Nb. Then, the ratio of useful width compared to the useless width is (Qh×Mb)/Lh≥(Qh×Mb)/Mb=Qh. Likewise, the ratio of useful height compared to the useless height is (Qv×Nb)/Lv≥(Qv×Nb)/Nb=Qv. Now, it's easy to know, under this proportionally partition mechanism, useful region of sampling rectangular is more than Qh×Qv times of that of the useless one. The Useful Proportion (UP) of the useful blocks is as below:  4 We can clearly and easily find a preliminary conclusion that: the bigger the Qh and Qv are, the higher the useful proportion is. If we partition the whole source image into more blocks, the proposed solution will definitely produce higher rate of useful region for sampling.

So, SR is the rate of the pixels extracted for sampling. And as Mp≤Mb and Np≤Nb, to gain the highest SR, Mp and Np should be as closer to the Mb and Nb respectively as possible.
SR≤ (Qh×Qv)×(Mb×Nb) M×N =UP(5)
When the patch coincides with its block completely, the whole image gets the highest rate of sampling. However, the computation will soar dramatically then. So, we advised an idea of the best solution to show the improvement direction. In practice, it can be adjusted according to the need.

To simplify the computation and associated work, we set Mb=Nb=Mp=Np=8, which means the patch region is same with its block so that we can get highest rate of pixels extracted for sampling SR. And we initiate the Rh and Rv to be 50%. Then for a normal 1280×720 HD image, Qh = |1280×50%|/8=80, and Qv = |720×50%|/8=45. The total quantity of patches is Qh×Qv=80×45=3600, the rate of pixels extracted for sampling is:
SR=( Qh×Qv) ×(Mp×Np)/(M×N)=25%
It shows that SR= Rh×Rv=50%×50%=25%, and UP=100%. So, when we choose proper values for Mb, Nb, Mp, Np, Rh and Rv, SR= Rh×Rv, and the proportion of useful blocks would be very high.

Patch-based Sampling and Reshaping

As the role of luminance for human being's perception, we calculate the average Y value of the block and add it to all the Y values of the patches in Y channel, so as to transfer the general information of the patches as well as the blocks. It isn't mandatory. And the data of each channel of the 8x8 sampled patch is transformed into 1-D vector whose length is 64. Three channels form a 192-long 1-D vector. 


Mean Subtraction and ZCA Whitening

To reduce the calculation and do normalization, we compute the mean value over all patches and subtract it to all the members of the 1-D vector. According to paper [4], the ZCA whitening patch-wise would reduce the correlation of the spatial representations, so ZCA whitening is used by this paper.


Preliminary concepts

After being preprocessed, the data is dealt with many hot IQA solutions: MS-SSIM, MAD, GSM, FSIM and FSIMc and so on. Then a good objective IQA method should be built.


VMAF

One of the most popular objective IQA methods is Video Multimethod Assessment Fusion (VMAF), which is researched by Netflix since 2014 and launched officially in 2018.

However, VMAF depends on subjective assessment, and its algorithm contains motion vector, which doesn't fit our need for unsupervised IQA. So, we refer to the key idea of VMAF: joint indices. YOLOv4

The machine learning model You Only Look Once (YOLO) offers a predicted way to know what the objects are and where they are at one glance. As the first one giving up pipeline completely, it detects the object with regression method by spatially separating bounding boxes and associated class probabilities. Then, a single neural network based on full images in one evaluation are used to predict them. Since YOLOv1 [21] is proposed in 2016, the amazing performance of YOLO families have surpassed the best Convolutional Neural Network (CNN) model "Faster R-CNN [22]" and developed fast. Until now, there is no other machine learning model is comparable with YOLO on object detection, and its latest version YOLOv4 [23] has achieved 65 FPS and accuracy of 43.5%, which gains the peak performance on both speed and precision. Thus, we choose YOLOv4. For GPU, it uses CSPResNeXt50/CSPDarknet53 in convolution layer. For VPU, it proposes group convolution without SE: it includes EfficientNet-lite/ MixNet/GhostNet/ MobileNetV3.

Image Quality Estimation

Unsupervised "Batched Learning" trains the linear decoder by provided preprocessed patches. The learned weights, the bias, and a mapping non-linear are all used to change the non-overlapping patches as reference and the images into sparse representations. These representations form a long vectors. Sparse coding-based methods generally perform optimization.


Validation

In the comparison of the quality estimators, we use the LIVE, the Multiply Distorted LIVE(MULTI), and the TID2013 (TID13) databases. Root mean square error (RMSE) assess the performance of the quality estimators, testing the accuracy, outlier ratio to indicate consistency, Pearson correlation, and Spearman correlation to display monotonicity. There are many ways with various histogram difference metrics (As Table 2), including Kullback-Leibler (KL) divergence, Earth Mover's Distance (EMD), histogram intersection (HI), Jensen-Shannon (JS) divergence, and L2 norm. The lower the difference, the better the estimator.  Among the IQA estimators above, AIVMAF performs better than former IQA indices, including UNIQUE and FSIMc. Its PLCC is generally higher than UNIQUE, and its RMSE is lower. These show that it produces better linearity and precision. Its outlier ratio is lower, so its data consistency is better. Its monotonicity is nearly same with UNIQUE because of their general similar sparse representation structure of estimation. And the speed of AIVMAF is faster than VSI.


Conclusion

This paper surpasses UNIQUE on simplification of extracting image features and preprocessing. We design creative "Proportional Partition" controlled by the horizontal and vertical rates of extracted pixel to balance the uneven distribution of image quality. We induce new methods inside the objective function when referring to VMAF and the contrast masking of DLM, though similar architecture of UNIQUE to train. We import VSI into the objective function to use saliency to enhance the indication of image quality. Contrast is used to KL-divergence as weight of each unit so that the estimation loss in higher contrast location produces worse estimation result; Vice versa, the effect of information loss with lower contrast will be relative weaken. This makes the algorithm closer to the HVS and produces smaller difference with subjective scores. To improve the framework, Yolov4 is introduced to make it more accurate and faster. "Batch Learning" accelerates the training. The test results show that AIVMAF does better than many recent methods. Its performance is among the best estimators of IQA.
