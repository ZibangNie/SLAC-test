Fig. S4 TGA curves of PEI and PEI-AuNPs with different amounts of PEI, Sample mass: ca. 10 mg; heating rate: 20 o C min -1 ; under N 2 flow rate of 80 mL min -1 ; in an aluminum pan.Thermal Decomposition Analysis To determine the amount of PEI coated on AuNPs, the TG/DTG curves of 100% PEI and PEI-AuNPs with different amounts of PEI are shown in Figure S3, respectively.It was found that the standard PEI was identified in three thermal events.The first mass loss was observed below 100 o C and is attributed to the vaporization of the moisture in the polymer chain.The second event which begins at 100 o C ends around 450 o C and is strictly related to the thermal decomposition of PEI (organic carbon) in the oxidizing under N 2 .This result agrees with thermal decomposition studies of PEI. 1-3The third event occurring above 450 •C is associated with CO and CO 2 formation due to intense heat (burning of the carbonaceous residue). 1, 4, 5Moreover, the TG curve of the PEI-Au1, PEI-Au2, PEI-Au3, PEI-Au4, and PEI-Au5 were investigated in Figure S3.All PEI-AuNPs sizes showed the % mass loss in three regions as well as standard PEI.However, the second event exhibited different % mass losses with different PEI-AuNPs sizes.The % mass loss in the 100-400°C range decreased with decreasing PEI proportion in the PEI-AuNPs from PEI-Au1 to PEI-Au5.Although water contents vary across samples, the gels or sediments obtained from the washing step contain different PEI contents but have the same mole/mass of Au 0 (assuming a complete reduction of Au 3+ ions to AuNPs/Au 0 /metal Au).The thermal stability of PEI over the 100-400°C range reflects residue masses of different PEI contents and AuNPs/Au 0 (that is constant).The consideration of Au 0 to the residual mass is negligible, inferring the PEI content in the gels/sediments indirectly.The result confirms that the amount of PEI affects PEI-AuNPs' sizes.
