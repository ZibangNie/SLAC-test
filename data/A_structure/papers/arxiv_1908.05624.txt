Here, we note that one might be able to think of a (perhaps, open) similar problem for a fiber product of locally trivial fiber spaces, not just for a direct product of topological spaces.In Appendix, we mentioned a simple example of a C([0, 1]; R)-manifold that cannot be embedded in the direct product (C([0, 1]; R)) n as a C([0, 1]; R)submanifold.
