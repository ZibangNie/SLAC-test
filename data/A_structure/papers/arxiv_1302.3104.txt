We call H the umbral algebra. The umbral calculus is the study of umbral algebra.

Let f (t) ∈ H. The smallest integer k for which the coefficient of t k does not vanish is called the order of f (t) and is denoted by O(f (t)) (see [11,12,16,17]). If O(f (t)) = 1, O(f (t)) = 0, then f (t) is called a delta, an invertable series, respectively. For given two power series f (t), g(t) ∈ H such that O(f (t)) = 1 and O(g(t)) = 0, there exists a unique sequence S n (x) of polynomials with g(t)(f (t)) k |S n (x) = n!δ n,k (this condition sometimes is called orthogonality type) for all n, k ≥ 0. The sequence S n (x) is called the Sheffer sequence for (g(t), f (t)) which is denoted by S n (x) ∼ (g(t), f (t)) (see [11,12,16,17]).
