However, the coefficients of variation of unmodified electrodes are an order of magnitude less than that of the modified electrodes. This finding suggests that there is little variability between the electrodes themselves; rather, the variability in Figures 2C, 4 and 5 is likely due to variability between selfassembled DNA monolayers. Ongoing work is aimed to improve the signal reproducibility with these monolayers.

We fabricated the electrodes by employing a binary self-assembled monolayer using 6mercapto-1-hexanol as a backfilling agent. While this is a common method for generating alkanethiol monolayers on gold, this strategy leads to monolayer heterogeneity. 1,2 This heterogeneity and noise in the measurement of these monolayers can be improved by forming DNA monolayers using ternary SAMs 3 and copper-free click chemistry. 4 The stability of binary SAMs is on the order of days-weeks 5 


Materials and Methods

The devices used to obtain the data in Figure 4 were measured three times prior to Cas12a treatment in attempt to decrease measurement variability. However, due to limited variability in consecutive, repeated measurements, only the first measurement before and after Cas12a treatment was used in analysis. All other data was generated by measuring the devices once before and once after Cas12a treatment. In Figure 5, the NTCs w ere pooled from the clinical sample data set and the analytical data set.


HIV Plasmid Preparation

The HIV Target DNA Plasmid: Starting from intact HIV8e5 virions (Seracare), genomic HIV RNA was extracted using the Qiagen Viral Mini kit. The gag gene was then amplified using the Agilent Brilliant II Core kit, with the reverse primer of 5'-GAATGACGTCcagttccttgtctatcggct-3' (the capital letters being an AatII restriction enzyme site, and the lower-case sequence indicating the reverse complement end of the gag p6 region) and the forward primer of 5'-CTGAGTCGACcagtattaagcgggggagaa-3' (capital letters being a SalI restriction enzyme site, and the lower case sequence indicating the gag g10 region). The amplified gag segment was inserted into the pGEM-Teasy vector in-between the SalI and AatII sites, thus creating the HIV target DNA plasmid. Prior to Cas12a assays, the target DNA plasmid was digested down to 3 linear segments with lengths 2564, 594, and 1122 base pairs with PvuII, and the products were purified with a standard phenol-chloroform extraction and ethanol precipitation protocol. The 1122 bp segment contains both the p24 and p7 cut sites for the Cas12a assays.


SWV analysis

To perform SWV, the Gamry potentiostat applies a staircase potential to the working electrode relative to the reference electrode. During one cycle, a forward pulse is applied and then, halfway through the cycle, the polarity is reversed. The current is sampled at the end of both the forward and reverse pulses. The software computes the difference between these two currents and plots the difference against the applied staircase potential. 7


Contamination prevention

To prevent amplicon contamination of neighboring wells, pipette tips were discarded after transferring LAMP amplicons from the tube to the electrode; one pipette tip was used per sample. Each electrode was sealed in its own petri dish for the one hour 37 o CRISPR incubation. After pipetting the LAMP amplicons on the electrode, the petri dish was sealed. The petri dish had an airtight seal, unlike conventional petri dishes.
