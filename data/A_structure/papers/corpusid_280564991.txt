Telitacicept plus low-dose mycophenolate mofetil in the treatment of IgA nephropathy: a retrospective study
2D57AB76A20A3912E5EB5517F2F7614C10.1007/s10238-025-01829-2Received: 6 June 2025 / Accepted: 22 July 2025Immunoglobulin A nephropathyTelitaciceptMycophenolate mofetilProteinuria
Presently, no specific therapies have been recognized for immunoglobulin A nephropathy (IgAN).Mycophenolate mofetil (MMF) has been verified effective for Chinese patients with IgAN.Telitacicept is a full-human TACI-FC fusion preventing B cells maturation and activation, and it has been proven to be beneficial for IgAN in a phase II clinical trial.This study was designed to observe the efficacy and safety of telitacicept plus low-dose MMF for IgAN treatment.This retrospective cohort study included 24 patients with IgAN, and patients were treated with telitacicept plus MMF.The primary outcome was settled as the changing in proteinuria and estimated glomerular filtration rate (eGFR).The subordinate outcome was set as the changing in hematuria.The mean follow-up time was 23 months.The median baseline proteinuria was 2.5 (1.74, 6.58) g/d, and eGFR was 94.97 (56.8, 120.67) mL/min/1.73m 2 .There were noteworthy reductions in proteinuria at 3, 6, 9, 12, 15, 18, 21 and 24 months when compared to the baseline levels [1.45 (0.78, 1.8) g/d [p = 0.0122], 0.505 (0.26, 0.99) g/d [p < 0.0001], 0.48 (0.28, 0.76) g/d [p < 0.0001], 0.3 (0.17, 0.85) g/d [p < 0.0001], 0.23 (0.18, 0.575) g/d [p < 0.0001], 0.18 (0.12, 0.325) g/d [p < 0.0001], 0.14 (0.105, 0.22) g/d [p < 0.0001] and 0.14 (0.103, 0.278) g/d [p < 0.0001]].All patients maintained stable eGFR during follow-up times.Besides, telitacicept plus MMF remarkably alleviated the hematuria.Telitacicept plus MMF treatment led to not only remarkable clinically significant reduction in proteinuria and hematuria, but also stable serum creatinine value of patients with IgAN without adverse side effects.

Introduction

Immunoglobulin A nephropathy (IgAN) is the most common type of glomerulonephritis worldwide, and is the dominant cause of end-stage renal disease (ESRD) [1].Up to 30% patients with IgAN progress to renal failure within 20 to 30 years after diagnosis requiring kidney transplant or dialysis [2,3].Clinical manifestations of IgAN range from proteinuria, asymptomatic hematuria, macroscopic hematuria and hypertension to progressive glomerulonephritis [4].Urine protein-to-creatinine ratio (UPCR) over 1 mg/mg, hypertension and decreased kidney function are the robust risks for ESRD [5].

The primary and codominant deposition of IgA1 especially galactose-deficient IgA1 (Gd-IgA1) in the mesangial area of glomeruli is the diagnostic basis for IgAN.Although the exact pathogenesis of IgAN remains unclear, the widely acknowledged pathogenesis of IgAN until now is "multi-hit hypothesis" including increased circulating levels of Gd-IgA1, production of autoantibodies targeting Gd-IgA1, circulating immune complex including Gd-IgA1 and Gd-IgA1-antiglycan IgG immune complex deposition in kidney [6].The immune complex in kidney leads to the complement system activation, the release of profibrotic and proinflammation factors, which contribute to the robust inflammation reaction, the proliferation of mesangial cell, tubular atrophy and interstitial fibrosis even crescents formation [3,7].Although Gd-IgA1 production has not been clearly elucidated yet, there are various researches demonstrating that cytokine B cell activating factor (BAFF) and a proliferation-inducing ligand (APRIL) concentrations which are involved with B cells maturation and activation, respectively, are positively correlated with Gd-IgA1 antibodies [3,8,9].Obviously, B cells play a pivotal role in the initiation and progression of IgAN.There exist no very recognized effective treatments for IgAN currently, while the standard care is approved widely including angiotensin-converting enzyme inhibitors (ACEi) and angiotensin II receptor blockers (ARBs), and they are the bedrock therapy for all patients.However, they are nonspecific and have no effect on reversing the progression of IgAN [10].Additionally, the nonimmunologic treatments also include sodium-glucose transporter 2 inhibition (SGLT2i) and endothelin receptor antagonism, but they are under clinical trials up to now [11,12].Therefore, the treatments targeting immunology are needed such as glucocorticoids and mycophenolate mofetil.Presently, the appropriate therapy for IgAN remains uncertain.

Telitacicept is a novel full-human TACI-FC fusion protein which combines with the B cell extracellular segment of the receptor TACI and Fc segment of IgG1.It prevents the combination between BAFF and APRIL binding to their receptors, which can block B cells maturation and activation to lessen autoantibodies production [9,13].There existing clinical trial shows that telitacicept can significantly reduce proteinuria of patients with IgAN [13,14].Mycophenolate mofetil (MMF), a potent suppressive medicine for B and T lymphocytes, can attenuate inflammation, tubulointerstitial fibrosis and glomerulosclerosis in kidney [15].Hou et al. have confirmed the effect of MMF on delaying the loss of estimated glomerular filtration rate (eGFR) and reducing proteinuria in patients with IgAN.However, the efficacy of MMF treating IgAN is still controversial [16].Proteinuria > 0.5 g/d, albumin excretion rate > 0.3 g/d and GFR < 60 ml/min/1.73m 2 are regarded as high risks for CKD progression [10,17].Therefore, it is necessary to identify therapeutic regimens which can simultaneously reduce proteinuria and stabilize kidney function.This study was designed to test the hypothesis that telitacicept plus MMF treatment would be efficacy and safety for patients with IgAN in reducing the risk of clinically important kidney outcomes in patients with IgAN and at the high risk of progression to ESRD.


Materials and methods


Study design and population

We retrospectively summarize the histologic and clinical data and the follow-up data for 98 patients from Renmin Hospital of Wuhan University, who were 18 years of age or older and had diagnosed with IgAN by kidney biopsy from 2021 to 2023.These eligible patients had a urinary protein level over 0.75 g/d and had eGFR over 30 ml/min/1.73m 2 of body surface area.The exclusive criteria were as follows:

(1) patients were secondary forms of IgAN; (2) other causes of chronic kidney disease such as systemic lupus erythematosus, diabetes mellitus, uncontrolled hypertension and malignancy; (3) patients had been treated with other immunosuppressants such as corticosteroids, hydroxychloroquine, cyclophosphamide and others; (4) an Oxford Classification of IgAN MEST or MEST-C score (M: mesangial hypercellularity; E: endocapillary hypercellularity; S: segmental glomerulosclerosis; T: tubular atrophy or interstitial fibrosis; C: crescent) is T2 or C2 which means over 50% tubule interstitial fibrosis or the present cellular or fibrocellular crescents over 25% of glomeruli [18,19].


Clinical, laboratory and pathological data

We collected the baseline clinical, laboratory and renal biopsy outcome of eligible patients retrospectively.The information of patients includes age, gender, blood pressure, hematuria, proteinuria, serum creatinine, eGFR, leukomonocyte and the score of pathology slides based on Oxford Classification of IgAN MEST-C score as exhibited in Table 1.


Treatment

We enrolled 24 patients with primary IgAN, and patients were treated with telitacicept and MMF.Patients received 160 mg subcutaneous telitacicept per week for 6 months.After 6 months of 160 mg treatment, patients with proteinuria levels below 0.3 g/24 h were administered a reduced dosage of 80 mg weekly for an additional 3 months.If proteinuria remained below 0.3 g/24 h during this period, the dosage of telitacicept was further adjusted to 80 mg every other week, which was maintained for at least 3 months.Meanwhile oral MMF 0.5 g, twice daily for 1 year followed by 0.5 g daily for at least 6 months.The efficacy was first censored at 6 months after treatment.And the therapies of patients were adjusted to 80 mg per week and oral MMF 0.5 g daily or suspending telitacicept according to patients' proteinuria, serum creatinine, eGFR, hematuria or adverse events.


Efficacy evaluation and endpoint

The endpoint was 30% reduction in eGFR, developing into end-stage renal disease (ESRD) or renal disease-related death.Efficacy evaluations were referred to proteinuria level and renal function (eGFR) during follow-up, which includes complete remission (proteinuria less than 0.3 g/d and eGFR reduction less than 30%), partial remission (proteinuria reduction more than 50% compared to baseline levels, but over 0.3 g/d with stable eGFR) and no response (failure to meet any criteria above) [1,20,21].The primary outcomes were settled as the reduction of proteinuria and the changing in eGFR.The subordinate outcome was set as the decline of hematuria from baseline, the alteration of the serum creatinine and blood lymphocytes change measured in follow-up time (3,6,9,12,15,18,21 and 24 months, respectively).Additional outcomes included the proportion of complete remission after 6 months treatment, the time to complete remission and the time of 50% proteinuria decline.Medicine adjustment could be made according to the treatment efficacy, and then we followed up all patients for a long time.


Adverse events

Adverse events during treatment were important to assess drugs' applicability.In our cohort, the adverse events included infection needing hospitalization, severe leukopenia, diarrhea, emesis and severe pain containing arthralgia, myalgia, headache and injection site reaction.

Primary outcomes

As shown in Fig. 2A, the decline of proteinuria was remarkably after 3 months of treatment.3A).Additionally, of all patients had stable serum creatinine value without deterioration, and there was no statistically significant difference during follow-up period (Fig. 3B).Finally, no difference was observed in the blood lymphocytes during follow-up time (Fig. 3G).Satisfyingly, as presented in Supplementary figure S1A, nine of 24 (37.5%)patients with telitacicept plus MMF treatment reached complete remission at 6 months (Supplementary figure S1A).During the final follow-up time, 20 of 24 (83.3%)patients had achieved complete response (Supplementary figure S1B).Impressively, all patients received 50% proteinuria decline within 10 months and the majority achieve 50% proteinuria remission within 6 months (Supplementary figure S1C).Remarkably, there were three patients with proteinuria over 10 g/d before treatment, which reached an average remission of 81.2% at the final follow-up time as displayed in Supplementary figure S1D.Additionally, as given in Table 2, telitacicept plus MMF treatment induced an average proteinuria remission rate of 87%.During follow-up times, none of the patients experienced a decline in eGFR exceeding 30%, progressed to ESRD, or succumbed to renal disease-related mortality.


Adverse events

Few adverse events were reported in our study.There were one case of arthralgia and one case of injection reaction (severe pain and mass formation) with telitacicept plus MMF treatment, but the symptom was eliminated when reducing telitacicept dose and rotating injection sites, respectively, and these did not cause treatment discontinuation.Infections such as pneumonia requiring hospitalization were not be observed during follow-up period.

In summary, the telitacicept plus MMF treatment could significantly reduce proteinuria and hematuria in patients with IgAN with well toleration.


Discussion

Patients diagnosed with IgAN are at high risk of end-stage renal disease.Patients with severe proteinuria have proved to get benefits from immunosuppression, and the combination of immunosuppression sometimes may achieve not only better outcomes, but also fewer side effects [10,21].In line with these reports, our research demonstrates the significant role of telitacicept plus low-dose MMF in mitigating the proteinuria, maintaining stable serum creatinine and noteworthily alleviating hematuria of IgAN patients when compared to the baseline before treatment.And the subsequent 24-month follow-up suggests that combination of telitacicept with low-dose of MMF leads to long-term remission of proteinuria and hematuria; meanwhile, stable renal function is also observed along the follow-up time.

The strongest prognosis factor of IgAN is proteinuria, and its effect is not only dose-dependent, but also independent of other risk factors [22].Although IgAN was firstly proposed by Dr. Jean Berger et al. more than 50 years ago and advances in the pathophysiology was made presently, the appropriate treatment especially immunosuppressive therapy for IgAN remains uncertain [23].Poorly glycosylated IgA1 causing IgA-containing immune complex formation and then deposition in kidney is the primary pathogenic feature of IgAN [24].According to Kidney disease improving global outcome (KDIGO) guidelines, optimized supportive therapy is suggested, and steroid is recommended to patients whose urine protein remains over 1 g/d after 3 months of treatment with supportive care [25].However, it has been proposed that patients (proteinuria ≥ 0.5 g/d) with IgAN at risk of progressive loss of kidney function should be treated in multiple ways simultaneously including prevention of IgA immune complex formation [26][27][28].Therefore, in our study, most patients have been treated with telitacicept combining with MMF without 3 months of supportive care alone previously which may be the contributor for the subsequent satisfied efficacy.Currently, mounting researches are targeting various disease pathogenesis processes of IgAN including complement inhibitors, BAFF/APRIL suppression, B cell depletion, plasma cell depletion or endothelin A receptor inhibition, which are all under investigation worldwide [29,30].According to current guidelines, as a Blys and APRIL dual inhibitor, the remarkable effectiveness of telitacicept in treating systemic lupus erythematosus has been proved [31].Otherwise, Zhang Hong and Jonathan Barratt et al. have reported that telitacicept reduces proteinuria through lowering both IgA-combined immune complex and circulating Gd-IgA1 [14,32,33].Previously, various guidelines recommended proteinuria below 1 g/d of IgAN patients as the treatment goal, but a recent cohort study including 1530 patients with IgAN discovers that patients with proteinuria levels <0.3 g/d had lower risk of kidney failure [34].Randomized phase 2 trial of telitacicept in patients with IgAN showed that 24-h proteinuria of patients treated with 240 mg/week could be decreased by 0.889 g/d (49% reduction from baseline), while those treated with 160 mg/week received 0.316 g/d reduction (25% reduction from baseline) at the 24-week treatment [14].Thus, combining telitacicept with other immunosuppressants is necessary to alleviate proteinuria.However, in this albeit retrospective study, a satisfactory proteinuria remission has been observed, in which telitacicept combining with MMF group decrease by 3.999 g/d (87%) from baseline.

Another immunosuppressive agent involved in our study is MMF which plays important role in immune system including suppressing dendritic activation, suspending the activation of T and B cell and inhibiting antibody production from B cells [35].The efficacy of MMF in IgAN has been observed in Chinese patients.Tang et al. reported that MMF was more effective in reducing proteinuria than prednisone therapy patients with over 2 g/d [36].Patients with persistent proteinuria (>1 g/d) treated with MMF could receive a 50% reduction in proteinuria [37].Recently, Hou and her colleagues conducted a randomized clinical trial for 8 years including 170 patients diagnosed with primary IgAN, and indicated that MMF markedly reduced the risk of the reduction in eGFR (2.9 (1.0) mL/min/1.73m 2 loss per year) when compared to patients discontinuing MMF (6.1 (1.2) mL/ min/1.73m 2 loss per year) [16].Different from the previous literatures above, we combined the telitacicept with MMF for patients with IgAN; additionally, our study apparently verified that the combining MMF and telitacicept (160 mg/ week) offered proteinuria remission more than 1 g/d decline at the 6-month treatment.Moreover, the telitacicept plus MMF therapy achieved 83.3% complete remission at final follow-up time.

Otherwise, comparing to proteinuria, hematuria has received less attention in IgAN treatment.As was reported by M.A et al., the persistence of microscopic hematuria might be a notable risk for end-stage renal disease (ESRD) specially occurring with proteinuria, and hematuria remission was related to improved kidney outcome in patients who diagnosed with IgAN [38][39][40].Tonsillectomy and steroid pulse therapy had been verified to contribute to hematuria remission of patients with IgAN in Japan [41].And a retrospective observational study from Japan showed that steroid could also alleviate hematuria alone [42].Additionally, Sonia Solomon et al. had observed that MMF could alleviate hematuria of children who diagnosed with IgAN [43].The research from Italy demonstrated that the methylprednisolone pulses and MMF could significantly mitigate hematuria in IgAN patients [44].In line with these previous reports, our study reveals that the telitacicept plus MMF therapy brings out brilliant effective on hematuria remission.Overall, the telitacicept combining with MMF therapy not only obtains satisfactory proteinuria and hematuria curative effects, but also maintains renal function stable.As is illustrated before, "multi-hit" hypothesis involving IgAN has been universally acknowledged; therefore, the application of immunosuppressants is necessary, and multi-target therapy may be a novel treatment for IgAN.

To more intuitively demonstrate the clinical benefit of the combination therapy in this study, as summarized in Table 2, we conclude the efficacy of other drugs reported in previous studies and telitacicept combined with MMF therapy for proteinuria remission in patients with IgAN.Several reports have demonstrated that RASS inhibitors result in approximately 30%-50% proteinuria reduction within 1 to 2 years [45].A pre-specified analysis of the DAPA-CKD trial including 270 IgAN patients shows that dapagliflozin, a SGLT2 inhibitor, can reduce the urinary albumin-to-creatinine ratio by 26% compared to placebo [46].According to KDIGO guideline, patients with IgAN are suggested to accept steroid when urine protein remaining over 1 g/d after 3 months optimized treatment.A clinical trial with 3.5 years of median follow-up time is conducted by Vlado Perkovic et al.Patients with IgAN have been treated with oral methylprednisolone for 0.4 mg/kg/d (the max dosage is 32 mg/d) for the first 2 months, which is followed by 4 mg/d taper each month, and the total course is 6-9 months.The methylprednisolone leads to approximately 28% proteinuria reduction compared to the control group, but the severe side effects especially infection restrict the application [5].A novel therapy for primary IgAN is targeted-release formation of oral budesonide which is confirmed to reduce 27% and 48% proteinuria compared with placebo at 9-month and 12-month, respectively [47].Hong Zhang et al. demonstrated that hydroxychloroquine (HCQ) made 50% patients with IgAN reaching 50% decrease in proteinuria, and the median percent reduction in proteinuria in HCQ group is 48.4% at 6 months [48].As a selective endothelin type A receptor antagonist, Atrasentan is verified to reduce proteinuria by 48% at 36 weeks [49].A randomized, controlled trial involving rituximab for IgAN treatment shows that rituximab is meaningless in alleviating proteinuria [50].Besides, iptacopan is a complement inhibitor targeting Factor B, and has been confirmed to achieve 23% and 38% reduction in proteinuria of patients' oral iptacopan (200 mg bid) at 3 months and 6 months, respectively [51].A randomized clinical trial conducted by Hou et al. has demonstrated that MMF treatment can achieve 57.1% proteinuria reduction when compared to the supportive group [16].Compared to reports above, telitacicept combining with MMF therapy seems to achieve more satisfactory effect on relieving proteinuria which has achieved 87% reduction in proteinuria as shown in Table 2.

We estimated the efficacy and safety of telitacicept plus MMF in patients with IgAN.Although our research was albeit retrospective, we provided a long-term followup setting primary outcomes and subordinate outcomes.

Long-term follow-up allowed us to track both the efficacy and short/long-term side effects of the telitacicept plus MMF treatment.In conclusion, our study seemed to get a better balance of drug efficacy and side effects.In our cohort, 83.3% patients treated with telitacicept and MMF achieved complete remission.As a suppressor for immunity, in a randomized phase 2 trail of telitacicept, serum immunoglobulin levels including IgA, IgG and IgM has been significantly reduced.However, it has not led to increased risk such as infection [14].Consistent with this report, the combination of telitacicept and MMF has not caused infection throughout the study.The limited financial resources of patients prevent the serum immunoglobulin collection in this study, but the stable serum lymphocyte is observed.Additionally, the distinctive renal function alteration has not appeared, which infers that the combination telitacicept with MMF does maintain renal function.However, it also may due to the patients enrolled present considerable renal function with average eGFR over 80 mL/min/1.73m 2 before the treatment; meanwhile, longer follow-up time is necessary and is beneficial to observe further change in renal function.

The study has several limitations.Firstly, the number of patients in our cohort can be expanded further and the follow-up, besides, a randomized trial may be more convictive.Additionally, the efficacy of telitacicept plus MMF therapy in IgAN was only observed in Chinese patients, which might exist racial differences in treatment.Therefore, whether this combination therapy is effective in severe proliferative lesion IgAN needs further exploration.

In conclusion, a cohort of Chinese patients with IgAN treated with telitacicept plus MMF could receive obvious proteinuria decline, significantly hematuria remission and stable eGFR.Telitacicept plus MMF may be an effective therapy option for patients with IgAN.
