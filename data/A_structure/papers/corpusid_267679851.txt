VERSION 1 -AUTHOR RESPONSE

Reviewer: 1 Dr. Jixiang Guo, Sichuan University Comments to the Author:

The author aimed to evaluate the effectiveness of the artificial intelligence in accelerating the definitive classification of nodules into malignant or benign.The movtivation is good, however, the proposed study should be refined.The detail concerns are provided as followed:

1.The author stated that the patients will be randomized into a standard-of-care (SOC) and an interventional arm, for the former, the multidisciplinary team will classify the nodule as benign, indeterminate, or malignant; for the later the results by AI will be known.This randomization is doubtful for the evaulation, the patients should be cross-enrolled to these two management strategies to sovle the objectives.

 We thank Dr Guo for the suggestion.Nonetheless, we are unsure of how our trial could be designed using cross-enrollement.Comparing two real-world strategies on head-to-head seemed to us as the best option..

2. Please state that how to obtain or define the "Gold Standard" for comparision, the "Gold Standard" was mentioned in the secondary objectives.Whether there is more senior radiologist who will give the Gold Standard result.

 The gold standard for deciding on the nature of a nodule (benign or malignant) is the definitive histology.In the absence of histology, it is the evolution of the nodule in chest imaging that makes it possible to decide between malignant and benign at the end of the 24 months of follow-up.This is stated in the "objectives and study design" paragraph 3. The specialization level of members of multidisciplinary team should be declared.Any duplication in personnel?

 Our trial is a "real-word" trial.In France, as required by the "haute authorité de santé", for thoracic oncology MDT: a thoracic surgeon, a radiologist, a radiotherapist, an oncologist and a pulmonologist (who may be the oncologist in the case of a pneumo-oncologist) must attend.

4. "……a 3 months reduction in the delay between lung nodule detection and its definitive classification into benign or malignant."How did the author get the conclusion of "3 months reduction" ?And the design the study.

 The 3 months interval is not based on trials.First, it is based on clinical pertinence as evaluated by thoracic oncologists in France.Also, the 3 months delay is the usual minimal interval between follow-up chest CT scans in patients with a suspicious nodule.
