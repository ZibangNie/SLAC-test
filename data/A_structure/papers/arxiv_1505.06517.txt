We investigate the discrete Sturm-Liouville problems −∆(p∆u)(n − 1) + q(n)u(n) = lw(n)u(n),where p is strictly positive, q is nonnegative and w may change sign. If w is positive, the ℓ 2 -space weighted by w is a Hilbert space and it is customary to use that space for setting the problem. In the present situation the righthand-side of the equation does not give rise to a positivedefinite quadratic form and we use instead the left-hand-side to definite such a form. We prove in this paper that this form determines a Hilbert space (such problems are called left-definite).

Main Result

Due to the fact that the sign of w is indefinite it is not convenient to phrase the spectral and scattering theory in the usual setting of a weighted ℓ 2 -space, since it is not a Hilbert space. Instead the requirement that q is non-negative but not identically equal to zero allows us to define an inner product associated with the left hand side of the equation Lu = wf giving rise to the term left-definite problem. H 1 = {u ∈ C N 0 : ∞ n=0 (p(n)|(∆u)(n)| 2 + q(n)|u(n)| 2 ) < ∞}
