. Resting-state functional connectivity associated with the square percept duration of the Coffer illusion for each seed region independently; p< .05, corrected. Note: Although the significant clusters of both seed regions look similar, they are not the same, which becomes visible at a lower statistical threshold. Figure S2. Geometric mean durations over all four trials for both percepts. In each trial, the global percept was perceived over longer periods than the local one. Importantly, durations do not differ significantly across trials for either percept. Error bars represent ±SEM. Figure S3. Control analysis using the median percept durations instead of the geometric mean.

Functional connectivity for the two seed regions revealed similar results for the IPS. For the insula, no significant clusters were observed; corrected (p <. 05). Circled areas were found independent of the type of data transformation.     Note: Zou's confidence interval is based on (Zou 2007).
