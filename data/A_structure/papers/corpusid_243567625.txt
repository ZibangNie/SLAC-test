Experimental Procedures

Materials. All amino acid derivatives involved in the synthesis were purchased from GL Biochem (Shanghai) Ltd. N, Ndiisopropylethylamine (DIPEA), O-benzotriazole-N,N,N',N'-tetramethyluronium-hexafluorophosphate (HBTU) were purchased from Fisher Scientific. The synthesis of all peptide fragments was based on solid-phase peptide synthesis (SPPS). The branched peptides were made via the combination of SPPS and liquid phase synthesis (Scheme S1). All crude compounds were purified by HPLC with the yield of 85%. All reagents and solvents were used as received without further purification unless otherwise stated. The baculovirus encoding H2B-RFP for cell transfection was purchased from Thermo Fisher Scientific (CAT#C10595). MitoTracker Green was purchased from Thermo Fisher Scientific. All antibodies (including Anti-H2B antibody and all secondary antibodies for immunofluorescence staining and western blot) were purchase from Abcam. Minimum Essential Medium (MEM) for HeLa, HepG2 and HEK293 cells culture were purchased from ATCC, fetal bovine serum (FBS) and penicillin/streptomycin from Gibco by Life technologies, and 3-(4,5-Dimethylthiazol-2-yl)-2,5-diphenyltetrazolium bromide (MTT) from ACROS Organics. Mitochondria isolation kit was purchased from Thermo Fisher Scientific, CAT#89874. The mitochondria were isolated according to the protocol provided by the company.

Instruments. All peptides were purified by Agilent 1100 HPLC system, equipped with an XTerra C18 RP column. LC-MS was operated on a Waters Acquity Ultra Performance LC with Waters MICRO-MASS detector. Transmission electron microscope (TEM) images were taken on Morgagni 268 transmission electron microscope. All samples were processed by negatively staining. Fluorescence images were taken by ZEISS LSM 880 confocal laser scanning microscope.


Determination of the critical micelle concentration (CMC).

Fluorescence characteristics of pyrene were determined by using Shimadzu RF-5301-PC fluorescence spectrophotometer. Emission spectra of pyrene were obtained by exciting the samples at 332 nm. All the MitoFlag solutions were prepared using PBS previously saturated with pyrene. The fluorescence intensity of the first (373 nm, I1) and the third (384 nm, I3) peak were recorded. The value of I1/I3 was plot against the concentration of MitoFlag in solutions. The concentration of MitoFlag at the first turning point of the curve is CMC.


Determination of dissociation constant (Kd).

The method for determining K d is derived from immunoprecipitation, a conventional method to quantify protein-protein interaction [1] . The binding complex formed by MitoFlag and NLS H2B is denoted as MitoFlag-NLS H2B . A number of solutions of MitoFlag (40 μM) were prepared. The solutions of MitoFlag (40 μM) were mixed with different amount of NLS H2B respectively, resulting in mixtures of MitoFlag (40 μM) and different concentration of NLS H2B (e.g. 20, 30, 40, 80, 100 μM). Since the binding between MitoFlag and NLS H2B leads to the formation of large aggregates ( Figure 2D), it is possible to isolate the unbound MitoFlag and NLS H2B from the MitoFlag-NLS H2B complex via the centrifuge of a rational speed ( Figure S14). Thus, highspeed centrifuge (12000 g, 30 min) was applied to the above mixtures and the supernatants were collected. The concentrations of MitoFlag and NLS H2B in the supernatants were determined by LC-MS with a Waters Acquity Ultra Performance LC and a Waters MICRO-MASS detector. The fraction of H2B-RFP in the nuclear and mitochondria of HeLa_H2B-RFP cells after the treatment of MitoFlag(L-D) is determined by the integral fluorescence intensity of RFP in mitochondria and nuclear in cell images ( Figure S12). Images were analyzed by ImageJ.

Cell culture. HeLa, HepG2, and HEK293 cells were cultured in Minimum Essential Medium (MEM) supplemented with 10% (vol/vol) FBS, 0.5% (vol/vol) penicillin (10, 000 unit), and 0.5% (vol/vol) streptomycin (10, 000 unit). All cells were maintained at 37 °C in a 5% CO 2 incubator.

Transfection. Cell transfection was performed according to the manufacture's manual. Briefly, baculovirus were mixed in culture medium (with 10% FBS). Following the manufacture's protocol, cells were directly incubated with virus in culture medium for at least 24 h. The incubation varies among different cell lines. Cells were then washed by fresh culture medium to remove virus remain and incubated at 37 °C in a 5% CO 2 incubator before further analysis.

Immunofluorescence. Cells were plated on confocal dishes (CellVis) and fixed in 4 wt% paraformaldehyde for 15 min and permeabilized with 1% BSA and 0.1% Tween 20. Fixed cells were incubated in primary antibody at 4 ℃ overnight, washed three times for 5 min each, incubated in secondary antibody for 1 h, washed three times for 5 min each.

Mitochondria isolation and separation. Mitochondria were isolated from cells treated by conditions of interest using mitochondria isolation kit (Thermo Fisher Scientific, CAT#89874) following the manufacture's instruction. The separation of mitochondrial outer membrane and matrix was performed following the steps in reference [2] .


Mitochondrial Western blots.

Mitochondria were isolated from cells treated by conditions of interest using mitochondria isolation kit (Thermo Fisher Scientific). Total mitochondrial protein extracts were prepared in lysis buffer (Cell Signaling Technology, with 1X protease inhibitor cocktail (Abcam)) followed by 5 freeze-thaw circles and 30 min sonication. Protein concentration was determined by Coomassie Blue method. Protein extracts (20 μg per lane) were separated by SDS-PAGE and transferred to polyvinylidene difluoride (PVDF) membranes. Western blotting was performed according to standard protocols.

: MitoFlag in MitoFlag-NLS H2B is determined by ([NLS H2B ] total -[NLS H2B ] free )/([MitoFlag] total -[MitoFlag] free ), where [MitoFlag] free and [NLS H2B ] free are the concentration of MitoFlag and NLS H2B in the supernatants, the [MitoFlag] total and [NLS H2B ] total are the total concentration of MitoFlag and NLS H2B in the mixtures. According to our experiment data, the value of n is around 1:1.25 which approximates to 1:1. Thus, to simplify the math, we set the binding ratio between NLS H2B and MitoFlag as 1:1. So, the K d was determined by [MitoFlag] free ×[NLS H2B ] free /[MitoFlag-NLS H2B ], [MitoFlag-NLS H2B ] is the concentration of MitoFlag-NLS H2B in the mixtures before centrifuge. [MitoFlag-NLS H2B ] = [NLS H2B ] total -[NLS H2B ] free . Finally, the K d values resulted from each mixture (MitoFlag + NLS H2B ) were averaged.
