METHODS

A 21-year-old obese female (body mass index: 31 kg/m²) presented with continuous urinary and fecal incontinence and bilateral lower limb weakness after a D11-L3 laminectomy for spinal teratoma a year ago.Examination revealed a patulous urethra.Videourodynamics showed a hyposensory bladder and intrinsic sphincter deficiency.

Due to obesity, perineal hypoesthesia, and challenges with per urethral CIC, a robotic Mitrofanoff appendicovesicostomy with a pubovaginal sling using an autologous tensor fascia lata graft was planned to enhance continence and facilitate easy CIC.The autologous graft minimized the risk of erosion, and rectus sheath harvesting was avoided due to her thick pannus and central obesity.

ABSTRACT

A 21-year-old obese female presented with urinary incontinence and lower limb weakness after a D11-L3 laminectomy for spinal teratoma.Evaluation revealed a patulous urethra with video-urodynamic study suggestive of hyposensory, hypocontractile bladder of 500 ml capacity with open bladder neck, intrinsic sphincter deficiency, and good compliance.Due to obesity, perineal hypoesthesia, and clean intermittent catheterization (CIC) challenges, a robotic Mitrofanoff channel and continent bladder neck (tensor fascia lata graft) were planned.Tension-free channel was achieved by removing one row of staples to increase channel length, use of indocyanine green intraoperatively to assess vascularity, and umbilicus as site of Mitrofanoff to avoid thick pannus.At 3-months' follow-up, the patient performed CIC comfortably every 5 h and was continent.

RESULTS

The total operative time was 180 min, with an estimated blood loss of 200 ml.Postoperative recovery was uneventful, and the patient was discharged on day 5 with a Foley catheter and infant feeding tube in the Mitrofanoff channel, both removed after 2 weeks.The patient subsequently performed CIC comfortably through the Mitrofanoff.At a short-term follow-up of 3 months, she remained completely continent and reported being highly satisfied with her ability to perform CIC with ease.


CONCLUSION

This video highlights the effectiveness of robotic-assisted Mitrofanoff appendicovesicostomy and autologous pubovaginal sling in achieving continence and improving quality of life in complex cases of obesity, paraparesis, and intrinsic sphincter deficiency.The meticulous preoperative planning, technical modifications, and use of intraoperative ICG dye ensured optimal outcomes, demonstrating how robotic precision and innovation can overcome the anatomical challenges in reconstructive urology.
