We are interested in the uniqueness of solutions of a nonlinear, pseudomonotone, stochastic diffusion evolution problem with homogeneous Dirichlet boundary conditions with reflection, where the noise term is additive and given by a stochastic Itô integral with respect to a Hilbert space valued cylindrical Wiener process.In fact, since there is no Itô formula available for a solution in general, a general uniqueness result seems not to be available.Nevertheless, assuming more regularity for the solutions or the reflection, we may show some comparison principles.

Introduction

We want to study uniqueness or, more precisely, comparison principles of solutions of the nonlinear stochastic obstacle problem with additive colored noise
           du − div a(•, u, ∇u) dt + f (u) dt = Φ dW + dη in Q T u(0, •) = u 0 ≥ 0 in D u = 0 on (0, T ) × ∂D u ≥ 0, η ≥ 0 and η(u) = 0,(P)
where the spatial domain D is a bounded Lipschitz domain in R d , T > 0 and Q T := (0, T ) × D. In this sequel, η plays the role of a reflection measure, i.e., η prevents u from becoming negative.The condition η(u) = 0 secures that the reflection is minimal, i.e., η is equal to 0 whenever u > 0. The exact meaning of the expression η(u) is given below.Problems of type (P) have been studied in the past decades in the deterministic case (see e.g.[3]) and for obstacles ψ of Lewy-Stampacchia type in [5,6,8].Stochastic obstacle problems with obstacles ψ of Lewy-Stampacchia type have been studied in [13,15].Therein, existence and uniqueness of a pair (u, η) solving (P) with obstacle ψ instead of 0 has been shown for different types of nonlinear operators and additive forcing f .The construction of the obstacle leads to the fact that the reflection measure η is a functional in L p ′ (0, T ; W −1,p ′ (D)), where p > 1 depends on the nonlinear operator and p ′ = p p−1 .In this case, an Itô formula for the equation in (P) is available, but this theory does not cover the case ψ ≡ 0. The case ψ ≡ 0 has been studied for d = 1 for linear operators − div a(u, ∇u) (see, e.g.[1, 7,9]).In those works, existence and uniqueness of solutions have been studied, where the uniqueness results in [1, 9] heavily rely on the fact that a linear problem is considered, the uniqueness result in [7] a-priori only holds for regular solutions.The uniqueness result therein has been generalized by establishing an Itô formula in [4].We mention that due to the fact that only one spatial dimension is considered, the solution u is continuous with respect to space-time.In higher spatial dimensions the solution fails to be continuous in general.As a consequence, an Itô formula as stated in [4] fails to hold in general which makes it difficult to obtain a uniqueness result in the general case (see Section 3).Moreover, the arbitrary spatial dimension makes it challenging to find a proper meaning to the expression η(u) since η is a non-negative random Radon measure with respect to Q T and therefore η is defined on Borel measurable functions only.Hence, a proper Borel measurable representative of a solution u has to be determined which is non-negative η-a.e. in Q T and satisfies η(u) := Q T u dη = 0. To this end, the theory of parabolic capacity (see e.g.[2,10,11]) has to be used.The existence of a pair (u, η) solving the obstacle problem (P) has been established in [14] under high regularity assumptions on the additive noise term.
