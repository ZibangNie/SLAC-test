Microbial rhodopsins are photoreceptive membrane proteins which transport various ions upon light absorption. Particularly, light-driven ion pump rhodopsins unidirectionally transport ions against concentration gradient. Whereas most ubiquitous pumps are outward H + pump, inward Cland outward Na + pumps are also known. Here, the ion transport mechanisms of ion pump rhodopsins are reviewed.
