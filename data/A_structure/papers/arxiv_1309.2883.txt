Disproving the conjecture on the structural physical approximation to optimal decomposable entanglement witnesses

We disprove the conjecture that structural physical approximations to optimal entanglement witnesses are separable states. The conjecture holds true for extremal decomposable entanglement witnesses.

(Some figures may appear in colour only in the online journal)

The basic idea of structural physical approximations (SPA) [1,2] is to mix a positive map with some relatively simple completely positive map C such that the mixture
= p − (1 − p) CP ,(1)
is completely positive. , being completely positive, can be realized in the laboratory and its action characterizes entangled states detected by an original positive map . In this paper, following [3], we take CP = 1l. A few years ago, the authors of [3] posed the following conjecture: structural physical approximations to optimal positive maps are entanglement breaking channels. In the language of entanglement witnesses, this conjecture may be reformulated as follows: let W be a normalized (i.e. Tr W = 1) entanglement witness (EW) acting on a Hilbert space
H = H A ⊗ H B of finite dimension D = d A × d B .
Consider the following convex combination
W (p) = pW + 1 − p D I,(2)
i.e. a mixture of W with a maximally mixed state I/D. It is clear that there exists the largest p * ∈ (0, 1) such that for all p p * , the operator W (p) 0 and hence it defines a legitimate quantum state. One calls W (p * ) the structural physical approximations of W [1,2]. The SPA conjecture [3] states that if W is an optimal EW, then its SPA W (p * ) defines a separable state. It is clear that if W (p * ) is separable, then for all p p * a state W (p) is separable as well. This conjecture was supported by numerous examples of witnesses (see, e.g., [4,5]), both decomposable and indecomposable, and it was also analyzed in the continuous variables case [5].

Recently, the SPA conjecture has been disproved by Ha and Kye [6], who have provided an example of an indecomposable optimal entanglement witness, whose structural physical approximation is entangled. The conjecture remains still open in the decomposable case (see recent discussion in [7][8][9]). In the present paper, we show that the SPA approximation to an optimal decomposable witness may provide an entangled state. Let us recall that a Hermitian operator W acting in
H A ⊗ H B is an EW if (i) ψ ⊗ φ|W |ψ ⊗ φ 0, and (ii) W 0, i.e.
W possesses at least one negative eigenvalue (see [10] for the recent review). The optimal witness is defined as follows: if W 1 and W 2 are two entanglement witnesses, then following [11] we call W 1 finer than
W 2 if D W 1 ⊇ D W 2 , where D W = { ρ | Tr(ρW ) < 0 },(3)
denotes the set of all entangled states detected by W . Now, an EW W is optimal if there is no other witness that is finer than W . One proves [11] that W is optimal if for any α > 0 and a positive operator P an operator W − αP is no longer an EW. The authors of [11] provided the following sufficient condition of optimality: for a given EW W define
P W = {|ψ ⊗ φ ∈ H A ⊗ H B | ψ ⊗ φ|W |ψ ⊗ φ = 0}. Now, if P W spans H A ⊗ H B , then W is optimal. An entanglement witness W is decomposable (DEW) if W = A + B ,(4)
where A, B 0 and B denotes the partial transposition of B. Clearly, if DEW W is optimal, then W = B and B is supported on a completely entangled subspace (CES) of H (a linear subspace ⊂ H is called CES if there is no non-zero product vectors in ). Now, if DEW W is optimal, then
W (p * ) = p * B + 1 − p * D I,(5)
which shows that SPA for an optimal DEW is a positive partial transpose (PPT) state (a state ρ is PPT if ρ 0). The SPA conjecture states that W (p * ) is not only PPT but is also separable. Since in 2 ⊗ 2 and 2 ⊗ 3 all PPT states are separable, the SPA conjecture is trivially satisfied for qubit-qubit and qubit-qutrit systems. In the general case, separable states define only a proper subset of PPT states, and hence one may have PPT states which are entangled. Now, we show that the SPA conjecture for optimal DEWs is not true. Consider the following family of bipartite Hermitian operators in
C 3 ⊗ C 3 W γ = 3B γ ,(6)
with
B γ = 1 − γ 2 P 10 + 1 − γ 2 P 20 + γ P 11 ,(7)
where P kl = | kl kl | denotes a set of rank-1 projectors with | kl = W kl ⊗I| 00 and W kl is a Weyl operator defined by W kl |i = ω k(i−l) |i−l with ω = e 2πi /3. Finally, | 00 = 1

denotes the maximally entangled state of two qutrits and γ is a real parameter. Hence B γ belongs to a class of Bell diagonal operators considered in [12]. One finds
| 10 = 1 √ 3 (|00 + ω|11 + ω * |22 ), | 20 = 1 √ 3 (|00 + ω * |11 + ω|22 ), | 11 = 1 √ 3 (|01 + ω|12 + ω * |20 ).

As a by-product, we showed that the three-dimensional subspace spanned by {| 10 , | 20 , | 11 } defines CES in C 3 ⊗ C 3 . Now, let us consider the SPA for W γ . To check for separability of W γ (p * ) we apply well-known realignment criterion [13]. The numerical analysis shows that at least for some range of γ the state W γ (p * ) is entangled. Figure 1 shows the plot of ||R(W γ (p * ))|| 1 − 1. Recall that if ||R(ρ)|| 1 > 1, then ρ is entangled.

Actually, in this case one can compute the realignment analytically (see the appendix). This way, the SPA conjecture is finally disproved. Interestingly, as was already shown in [5], the conjecture is true for a proper subset of optimal decomposable entanglement witnesses, i.e. witnesses of the of the form W = P and P = |ψ ψ| with entangled |ψ .

To summarize: we have found a counterexample disproving the SPA conjecture for optimal decomposable entanglement witnesses. The idea of using the family W γ comes from [12], where the Bell diagonal states with bound entanglement were considered. Since W γ can be always embedded in C m ⊗ C n for m, n 3, the SPA conjecture is disproved also in the general case. The only open problem is to (dis)prove it for C 2 ⊗ C n with n 4. It should be stressed that the SPA conjecture holds true for extremal decomposable entanglement witnesses, i.e. witnesses of the form W = |ψ ψ| . This result was already derived in [3].
