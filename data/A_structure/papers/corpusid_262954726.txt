Accurately weigh 0.300 g of dry modified lignosulfonic acid into a 250 mL Erlenmeyer flask, add 25 mL of 0.100 M NaOH, ultrasonically dissolve it and stir for 1 h to make it fully react. Then phenolphthalein is used as an indicator to titrate the excess NaOH with 0.100 M hydrochloric acid until the brick red just disappears. The volume of HCl consumed was recorded as V1, and the average of three parallel titrations was taken. At the same time, the same operation was performed with unmodified sodium lignosulfonate, and the volume of HCl consumed was recorded as V2. The actual degree of substitution (DS) was calculated according to formulas (1) and (2). 1) The DS calculation formula is as follows:
