ASSESSING THE ACCURACY OF FIB-4 SCORES TO POINT SHEAR WAVE ELASTOGRAPHY IN MONITORING DIFFERENT STAGES OF FIBROSIS IN NON-ALCOHOLIC LIVER DISEASE

BackgroundNon-alcoholic liver disease (NAFLD) assessment is done by measuring biochemical parameters from blood samples.However, non-invasive methods have gained significance lately.Biochemical parameters and staging of fibrosis-associated NAFLD based on the levels of biomarkers have been established procedures in the field.So, the utilization of non-invasive methods as a comparable means for fibrosis staging in NAFLD has yet to be established.ObjectivesTo compare the diagnostic performance of serum markers to point shear wave elastography for classifying fibrosis in NAFLD and its staging.Materials and MethodsData were collected by laboratory tests and point shear wave elastography.A comparison of FIB-4 indexes scores and point shear wave elastography for the staging of fibrosis in NAFLD was made based on crosstabulation of the quantitative data obtained.ResultsA positive and significant correlation between FIB4 and age, ALT, AST, RBS, AST/ ALT, AST/ UL AST, APRI, AST/ Platelet count, and point shear wave elastography (PSWE).The correlation was negative and nonsignificant between FIB4 and Weight, Height, BMI, UL AST, TSB, and Platelet count.ConclusionThe study observed a positive correlation between FIB-4 and PSWE.Thus, the present study proposes the use of PSWE as a non-invasive diagnostic tool in the staging of fibrosis in NAFLD for patients with an intermediate FIB-4 score.

INTRODUCTION

Numerous people have liver fibrosis without a known liver sickness that has been viewed as related to NAFLD, which might progress to non-alcoholic steatohepatitis (NASH) (1) .NAFLD is a term used for collecting liver symptoms in individuals not drinking alcohol.NAFLD is characterized by excessive amounts of fat in liver cells.The damage caused is comparable to the liver damage brought about by alcohol usage (2)   .The increase in NAFLD cases in the population is associated with the rise in type 2 diabetes.In people who are not obese, the pervasiveness of NAFLD might be low.While NAFLD is relatively harmless, patients suffering from NASH develop fibrosis followed by liver cirrhosis.These patients usually do not display any symptoms till the end stage of liver sickness (3) .NAFLD incidence rate has constantly increased throughout the years and is the most widely recognized liver sickness in Western nations (4) , of the total cases reported, only 3%-5% of the biopsies for NASH in the US advance to cirrhosis, liver dysfunction, and cancer of the liver.NASH-related cirrhosis is the third most prominent cause of liver transplantation.

Non-invasive liver tests (NILTs) are separated into three classes which are direct serum markers, backhanded or basic serum markers, and different modalities of imaging for the separation of NASH from high-level hepatic fibrosis and straightforward steatosis.Serological tests have likewise been laid out to identify liver fibrosis and cirrhosis.Point shear wave elastography is a creative technique that depends on the waves applied to a demonstrative ultrasound framework (5) .With the loss of liver fat with fibrosis/ cirrhosis, ultrasound may miss the detection of early stages of fibrosis.Elastography could be useful in measuring the levels of fibrosis (6) .

Point shear wave elastography (pSWE) is a recently developed technique.It can be used with highend ultrasound-based equipment.It allows B-mode ultrasound guidance of elastography measurements (7)   .Fibrosis prediction in chronic liver diseases by FibroTest is a commonly used approach.However, it has limitations in predicting conditions such as acute hepatitis, acute hemolysis, inflammation, or extrahepatic cholestasis.To overcome these limitations, scoring systems based on non-invasive methods are routinely used in clinical labs.These include methods such as the FIB-4 index (screening index for NAFLD based on ALT, AST, platelet count, and age), NFS (NAFLD fibrosis score), and BARD score for predicting advanced fibrosis in NAFLD (8,9) .FIB-4 and NFS are widely used non-invasive methods for screening liver fibrosis.The present study also aimed to analyse and compare serum markers to point shear wave elastography in NAFLD.


MATERIALS AND METHODS

This is a cross-sectional study that was conducted at a private ultrasound clinic in Sulaymaniyah from July 2019 till the covid 19 pandemic which caused a delay in the research process.The sample of patients consisted of 50 patients who were diagnosed with NAFLD via ultrasound and blood tests, all alcoholic patients, patients with viral and autoimmune hepatitis, Wilson's disease, and hemochromatosis were excluded.The output from semi-structured questionnaires was Statistically evaluated by SPSS software, and data from open-ended questionnaires were analysed thematically.The analysis method used for the study was cross-tabulation.Cross-tabulation is used to measure the relationship between multiple variables.It is a quantitative method.


RESULTS

The following results were obtained from the biochemical parameters analysis: The mean value of ALT was 70.47, mean AST was 54.04, ULAST was 25.4,TSB was 1.0, ALP was 129.23, RBS was 123.4,Platelet count was 218.8, AST/ALT was 0.87, AST/ ALT was 0.87, AST/ ULAST was 2.3, APRI was 1.06, AST/ Platelet count was 0.25, Age * AST was 2624.66,ALT was 7.95, Platelet * ALT was 1764.03,mean TIBC (total iron binding capacity) was 284.66,PSWE was 4.25 Kpa, IQR/MED was 42.65, AST/ALT reference was 0.7, APRI reference was 0.5 and PSWE reference was 4.5 Kpa, as shown in table 2.

The following correlations were found between FIB-4 and several variables, as shown in table 4: A positive and significant correlation between FIB4 & Age, ALT, AST, RBS, AST/ ALT, AST/ UL AST, APRI, AST/ Platelet count, and PSWE.The correlation was negative and non-significant between FIB4 and Weight, Height, BMI, UL AST, TSB, and Platelet count.The correlation was positive and non-significant between FIB4 and ALP, ALT, PLT * ALT, and TIBC.


DISCUSSION

The positive correlation between FIB4 and age, ALT, and AST has been established even by other studies (10,11)   .There is no clear-cut correlation between BMI, platelet count, and APRI in the literature for the establishment of NAFLD (8,11) .The sensitivity of FIB-4 for predicting liver fibrosis was comparable to or better than APRI.The study also provided details related to optimal cut-off values for different stages of fibrosis for both serum markers.The diagnostic accuracies of APRI and FIB-4 for prediction were compared with APRI and FIB-4 accompanying ultrasound SWE (shear wave elastography) (12) .

Owing to the increase in cases of NAFLD and newer therapeutic options, it is necessary to have methods that are not invasive for the identification and monitoring of the population, unlike liver biopsy.In the present study, PSWE is observed to be a sensitive and reliable method for staging NAFLD and is comparable to FIB4-based staging.A meta-analysis for the evaluation of PSWE in fibrosis staging in NAFLD has observed similar results.Analysis of nine studies done using PSWE and their evaluation showed that the method is extremely useful for staging NAFLD (11) .

In conclusion, here are a significant number of false positives and false negatives with FIB-4 in general and in the high-risk population.Though FIB-4 is the correct way to study fibrosis, researchers must use other methods to compare with FIB-4 such that better conclusions can be reached.In the present study, FIB-4 cut-offs are helpful for the identification of various fibrosis stages in NAFLD; however, this method could not identify any patients at stage 4. Similarly, PSWE also could identify patients at different stages, excluding stage 4.There was a good correlation in identification when FIB4 indices and PSWE were compared, and both were effective in detecting NAFLD till stage 3. Hence, FIB-4 scores are quite accurate and comparable to PSWE in the staging of fibrosis in NAFLD patients.
