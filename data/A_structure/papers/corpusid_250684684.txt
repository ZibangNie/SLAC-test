PREFACE

In conjunction with the establishment of Aerospace Society Malaysia (AEROS Malaysia) in January 2017, this inaugural edition of the biennial AEROS Conference series is organized as the key platform for local and international aerospace communities to come together and share their recent researches in development and progress of the aerospace field. Moreover, the conference also aims to become the catalyst for potential collaborations between the academia and the aerospace government agencies and industry players, which is one of the main objectives for the establishment of AEROS Malaysia.

This first AEROS Conference is held in Putrajaya, Malaysia on 12th December, 2017. After extensive peer review process, 39 paper submissions have been accepted into the conference and included in the conference proceedings. The selected papers cover a wide range of aerospace topics such as structures and materials, aerodynamics, flight dynamics and control, propulsion, aerospace application and other related areas.

I like thank the conference participants for their contributions and the conference reviewers for their expertise and valuable comments. I also like to thank the conference organizing committee for their commitment and tremendous support to the success of this inaugural AEROS Conference 2017.


Dr. Fairuz Izzuddin Romli


Chairman of AEROS Malaysia Editor of AEROS Conference 2017 Proceedings
