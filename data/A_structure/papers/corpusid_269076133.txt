As China's foreign trade continues to progress, a trade surplus in inland container transportation has emerged.At the same time, businesses need to not only focus on transportation tasks but also prioritize customer service requirements.To address the issues of container supply shortages and inefficient empty container allocation, this article establishes a container truck scheduling model that includes multiple terminals and yards.With the objective of minimizing total costs and maximizing customer satisfaction during the allocation process, the model incorporates coupling constraints between vehicle flow and container flow.Instance verification using Cplex demonstrates that the proportions of different cost types and the range of expected time windows can impact the formulation of transportation plans and, consequently, total costs.Lastly, numerical experiments are conducted to analyze the cost variations and transportation plan preferences under different ranges of time windows and different weights assigned to transportation costs and fixed costs.

Introduction

The 14th Five-Year Plan formulation proposed the development goal of "accelerating the formation of a new development paradigm featuring dual circulation, in which domestic and overseas markets reinforce each other, with the domestic market as the mainstay", which greatly promoted the development of China's domestic container transportation industry, but the problems of low utilization rate of empty containers in inland container transportation and low allocation efficiency greatly increased the container trucking costs.On the other hand, with the continuous increase of global freight volume and the continuous improvement of logistics services, improving customer satisfaction has become a problem that container transportation enterprises must face and solve.

Current research can be divided into three categories.The first is to consider the scheduling problem of inland container trucks with empty container allocation.Zhang et.al. [1,2] proposed the asymmetric multi-travel salesman sales problem with time window including empty container repositioning.Sterzik and Kopfer [3] redefine empty box resources and explicitly apply them to problem modeling.The second is the container truck scheduling problem considering customer satisfaction. [4]Wang et al. [5] determined customer satisfaction by three factors: delivery time, commodity turnover, and service quality, converted customer satisfaction into a penalty function, and transformed the two-objective model of cost minimization and customer satisfaction maximization into a single objective.Qin et al. [6] developed a comprehensive model considering carbon emission cost and customer satisfaction, using on-time delivery to judge customer satisfaction, and taking the ratio of cost to customer satisfaction as the objective function.The third is the multi-objective container truck scheduling problem considering customer satisfaction.Shao et al. [7] converted customer satisfaction into a fuzzy function in the multimodal transportation problem, and at the same time, taking into account shippers' requirements for transportation cost, timeliness, reliability and flexibility, built a multi-objective multimodal transportation problem model taking into account customer satisfaction.Sun and Li similarly describe the problem as a multi-objective fuzzy mixed integer nonlinear model.


Problem Statement


Problem description

In inland container transportation, there are four types OF containers involved in import and export: import full container (IF), import empty container (IE), export full container (OF), and export empty container (OE).Here we distinguish between two types of customers: shippers and recipients.The shipper provides the goods shipped through the dock to the foreign area, while the receiver obtains the goods shipped from the dock.Due to the imbalance in container demand caused by import and export tasks, the empty boxes need to be relocated after the completion of each transportation task and the empty boxes are transferred to the storage yard, that is, the storage place of the empty containers.To complete all the transportation tasks while balancing the distribution of empty containers in the transportation system, we need to import or export empty containers.

Problem Assumptions

1.There is only one dock in the system.

2. All trucks and containers are homogeneous, with one truck carrying one container at a time.

3. Each customer node can be accessed only once.4.There is plenty of space for empty containers in the yard.

5. All trucks depart from the yard /ICD and eventually return to the yard /ICD, but do not need to return to the original yard /ICD.

6.All terminals and customer locations have time window limits, the entry full container and the exit full container have the departure and destination time window, the entry empty container has only one start time window, the exit empty container has only one destination time window.

Computational Study


Influence analysis of weight parameters

The selection of weight parameters will affect the solution results of the model.The results are shown in Table 4.In this paper, two cases of w 0.1 and w 0. 5 are considered to carry out numerical experiments.(w w 1) When the value of w is smaller, the sum of transportation cost and fixed cost takes a smaller proportion in the total cost; conversely, the penalty cost of customer delivery satisfaction takes a smaller proportion in the total cost.As can be seen from the table, when the weight of transportation cost and fixed cost is different, the total cost of small time window is always higher than that of large time window.This is because when the limitation of the customer's expectation time window is large, the requirements of the customer are less easily met, and the probability of the container truck arriving at the customer is greater when the time is not in the expectation time window, and the penalty cost of the customer is higher.

Figure 2 shows the cost changes of transportation and fixed cost and weighted total cost under different time windows and weights of transportation and fixed cost in different ranges, where the horizontal coordinate is the proportion of transportation and fixed cost in total cost, the left vertical coordinate is the total cost of transportation and fixed cost, and the right vertical coordinate is the total cost.The analysis shows that the large time window has an earlier decline trend than the small time window.Under the condition of large time window, when the weight of transportation cost and fixed cost is 0~0.4, the difference between transportation cost and fixed cost is not big, when the weight is 0.5, there is a big gap with the previous cost, and then it shows a downward trend.However, under the condition of small time window, when the weight of transportation cost and fixed cost is 0~0.8, the difference between transportation cost and fixed cost is not much, and when the weight is 0.8, there is a cliff-like decline.


Time window effect analysis

In this section, a representative small scale example is selected to analyze the sensitivity of the impact of time window on cost and the range of change.

When the time window is large, with the increase of w , there will be obvious changes in both vehicle transportation cost, fixed cost and customer penalty cost, and when w reaches 0.8~0.9,there will be almost no change.When the time window is small, the change is not significant when the value of w is 0.1~0.7,but the change is very obvious when the value of w is 0.7~0.9.It shows that the sensitivity of the cost range of large time Windows is greater than that of small time Windows.This paper also selected a large-scale example for analysis, as shown in Figure 3.It is found that the sensitivity of the cost variation range of large time window becomes larger, and the sensitivity of the cost variation range of small time window becomes smaller, and thus proves the correctness of the sensitivity analysis above.Overall consideration, in the transportation task, the transportation company should not only consider the quality of transportation completed within the customer time window, but also can not ignore the impact of transportation costs and fixed costs.


Conclusion

This paper establishes an inland container transportation network with multiple docks and yards, studies the scheduling of containers and trucks in the network, establishes a multi-objective scheduling model, and verifies the model with the real data of a container transportation company.The results show that the proportion of transportation cost and fixed cost in the total cost and the range of customer expectation time window will affect the total cost.Further, according to the range of customer expectation time window, this paper designs two kinds of time window examples under multiple sets of the same basic data, and analyzes the weight of transportation cost and fixed cost and the range of expected time window respectively in the calculation examples.Finally, it is found that the total cost with a wide range of expectation time window is always lower than the total cost with a small range of expectation time window.With the increase of the proportion of transportation cost and fixed cost in total cost, the change trend of the two kinds of costs is opposite, and the expectation time window range is large, and the change trend is early.In addition, when transportation costs and fixed costs account for 30% to 50% of the total cost, it is the best decision to ensure that customer needs are met and the total cost is in a reasonable range.

Future studies can be more closely combined with reality, such as considering the uncertainty of the traffic environment or the uncertainty of transportation time and demand.Another research direction is to solve larger scale scheduling problems by designing heuristic algorithms such as variable neighborhood search algorithm.
