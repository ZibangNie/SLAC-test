## Interpretation and stopping boundaries

The per-query extrema describe this set of saved, single-generation predictions. They are a **permissive observed envelope**, not a bound on expected model quality or an achievable global relation policy. Different questions' extremizing masks may conflict on the same shared static edge. Generation randomness, exact-response inheritance and the already exposed sample further limit interpretation.

Full-adjacency versus I can show the behavior of the frozen structural rule; it does not test the accuracy or value of learned JEV relations. If adjacency helps, content and fixed-placebo controls are still needed. If no reachable observed pack improves a question, this limits the present realization, not every generator or future selector. The separate demand-compilation experiment concerns fixed-oracle access equivalence and does not supply missing relation labels, preserve the original placebo statistics automatically, or authorize this answer stage.

Raw questions, answers, per-question identities, evidence text, provider bodies and credentials remain in ignored local artifacts. The public release requires all planned comparisons, full known/unknown/reserved accounting, source hashes and independent numerical verification. No credentials or private data are included in commits.
